Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Darts Throw Analysis ("the app") is a tool for recording a dart-throw video and getting AI-based coaching feedback on your technique. This page explains what data the app collects and how it's used, for both the mobile app and the web app.

What we collect

  • Video you record or choose. When you record a throw with your camera, or pick an existing video, that file is uploaded to our server so it can be analyzed. We don't access your camera, microphone, or media library for any other purpose, and we don't collect video in the background.
  • Analysis results. The scores and feedback generated from your video (elbow stability, wrist snap, etc.) are stored alongside the video so you can revisit them without re-analyzing.
  • Nothing else. We don't collect your name, contacts, location, or device identifiers, and we don't use analytics or advertising SDKs.

How your video is used

Uploaded videos are processed on our server using an on-device pose-detection model (MediaPipe) to compute throw-mechanics scores. Videos and their results are stored on our hosting provider only for as long as needed to show them back to you in the app's library, and you can delete any video yourself at any time from the library screen. We don't sell or share your videos with third parties.

Saving videos to your device

The "download" option in the video library saves a copy of your video to your device's own photo/video gallery. That copy is then entirely under your device's normal storage and permissions — we have no access to it once saved.

Purchases

The optional lifetime-unlock purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We don't receive or store your payment details — Google handles the transaction and simply tells the app whether the purchase succeeded.

Permissions the mobile app requests

  • Camera & microphone — to record a throw video from within the app.
  • Photos/videos (gallery) — to let you pick an existing clip, or save an analyzed one back to your device.
  • Internet — to upload your video and fetch analysis results.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
